      • Two machines; Experiment 3 adds Sam to the two-member workload. Alex starts one second before Casey arrives. The replay clock starts at Casey’s arrival.

Only Experiment 4 adds walking time. Sam arrives at second 3 in Experiment 3 and alongside Casey in Experiment 4.
      • Each participant exercise occupies a machine for two minutes, followed by one minute of recovery if another exercise remains. Within-exercise sets and rest are aggregated.
      • Alex is an independent nonparticipant. His name and actions are visible to you as the experiment observer, not as a declared plan to the scheduler.
      • Both label views agree here because sensing is immediate; neither reserves a free machine for a walker. Predictions are not shown as actual release times.
      • This explains S05 and S43. Other spreadsheet scenarios are not implemented in this interface yet. Experiment 2 adds configurable refusal probability. Experiment 3 adds one competing member; Experiment 4 adds competing advice during walking; delayed sensing remains future work.
